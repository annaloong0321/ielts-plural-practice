WOMAN: Jack, I'm thinking of taking the kids to the seaside on a surfing holiday this summer and I wanted to ask your advice — as I know you're such an expert.

JACK: Well, I don't know about that, but yes, I've done a bit of surfing over the years. I'd thoroughly recommend it. I think it's the kind of holiday all the family can enjoy together. The thing about surfing is that it's great for all ages and all abilities. My youngest started when he was only three!

WOMAN: Wow. So where did you go for your holiday?

JACK: We went to the west coast of Ireland. The weather's not always ideal, but the surf conditions are fantastic.

WOMAN: And was it expensive?

JACK: Not too bad. The flights cost about £90 per person return, and we rented a small apartment near the beach for around £400 a week. But that's in peak season — it's cheaper if you go earlier in the summer.

WOMAN: Right. And what about the surfing lessons?

JACK: We booked a five-day course with a local surf school. It cost about £180 per person, which included all the equipment — wetsuits and boards. The instructors were great, really patient with the kids.

WOMAN: That sounds good. And what age do they start teaching?

JACK: They take children from eight, but they have to be able to swim, obviously.

WOMAN: That's fine. My youngest is nine and she's a strong swimmer. So when did you go?

JACK: We went in July. The water was actually quite warm — well, relatively speaking. You still need wetsuits, but the sea temperatures can reach 15 or 16 degrees in summer.

WOMAN: Right. And is there anything else to do in the area, if the children get tired of surfing?

JACK: Oh, there's loads. There are some great walking trails along the cliffs, and a few castles and ancient sites to explore. And the local seafood is fantastic.

WOMAN: Sounds perfect. Well, I think you've convinced me.

JACK: Good. Now, one piece of advice I would give you is to book early. The surf school gets very busy, especially in the summer holidays.

WOMAN: Right. When do you think I should book?

JACK: I'd suggest at least three months in advance. We left it quite late and almost couldn't get places.

WOMAN: OK. I'll look into it tonight.

JACK: And make sure you get travel insurance that covers surfing. Some policies don't include it.
